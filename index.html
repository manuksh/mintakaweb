Why Mintaka

Mintaka LLC focuses on FPGA design, SDR design, and verification for demanding engineering programs.

Our engineers take projects from architecture and RTL through simulation, hardware bring-up, and coverage closure. Mintaka-AI is our separate branch developing AI tools for engineering automation.

FPGA Design

Reusable RTL, FPGA IP integration, CDC/reset strategy, and timing-aware implementation for AMD/Xilinx and Intel platforms.

Verification

Coverage-driven SystemVerilog, UVM, assertions, cocotb, scoreboards, and regression flows that expose bugs early.

SDR Design

Digital down/up conversion, filters, synchronization, modulation, FEC, packet processing, and FPGA-based RF pipelines.

AI Tooling Branch

Mintaka-AI, our separate branch, develops AI-assisted tools that support documentation, verification, and engineering workflows.
