Mintaka Company Positioning

Core Positioning
Mintaka is an SDR, SatCom and FPGA engineering company.

We provide specialized engineering services in FPGA design and verification, SDR and satellite communication systems, signal-processing research, communication-system modeling, data analysis and hardware prototyping.

The company is not positioned as a generic outsourcing or staffing provider. Mintaka works as a specialized engineering and R&D partner, taking clearly defined technical problems and delivering verified, measurable engineering results.

What Mintaka Sells
Our primary commercial service is FPGA Design & Verification. This is the near-term revenue engine of the company.

Mintaka can take complete engineering work packages covering RTL/IP verification, verification planning, testbench development, assertions, regression, coverage, bug analysis, FPGA implementation support and hardware validation.

Alongside verification, Mintaka provides:
• SDR & SatCom engineering
• communication-system research and modeling
• DSP and adaptive signal-processing algorithm development
• channel modeling and emulation
• engineering data analysis and applied statistics
• rapid SDR/FPGA prototyping
• hardware-based validation and experimentation

Research is a Service
Mintaka does not need to sell a finished modem, satellite payload or complete communications product. We can sell engineering research itself.

A customer can bring a technical question or problem. Mintaka builds the appropriate model or experiment, runs simulations and measurements, analyzes the resulting data and provides an engineering conclusion, recommendations, algorithms or prototype.

For example, we can investigate how a communication link behaves under noise, Doppler, interference, fading or synchronization errors; compare receiver algorithms; determine operating boundaries; develop an adaptive algorithm; and validate the result statistically and on SDR/FPGA hardware.

The customer buys the engineering result and reduced technical risk. Models, simulation infrastructure, analysis tools and AI-assisted workflows are the machinery Mintaka uses to produce that result.

SatCom as the Technology Showcase
Satellite communications is Mintaka's flagship technical domain and technology showcase.

SatCom naturally combines difficult engineering problems: weak signals, Doppler, synchronization, coding, interference, limited power, RF constraints, DSP and FPGA real-time processing. Demonstrating capability in this domain provides strong evidence of Mintaka's engineering depth.

However, Mintaka is not limited to satellite companies. The same FPGA verification, SDR, DSP, modeling and prototyping capabilities can be provided to telecom, aerospace, defense, industrial and other technology companies.

Therefore the message is:

Mintaka specializes in SDR, SatCom and FPGA engineering — while our FPGA verification and engineering services are available to a much broader technology market.

Mintaka SDR Platform
Mintaka is building a reusable SDR research and prototyping platform rather than a single end product.

The platform combines reusable communication blocks, reference algorithms, channel models, SDR hardware, FPGA implementations, verification infrastructure and measurement/data-analysis tools. It allows the engineering team to assemble experiments and prototypes quickly for new customer problems.

The platform can include:
• modulation and demodulation reference chains
• synchronization algorithms
• adaptive filters
• interference cancellation
• weak-signal detection
• coding/decoding experiments
• channel models
• Doppler and frequency-offset models
• SDR links
• FPGA DSP blocks
• test and verification environments
• statistical analysis and performance measurement

The SDR Link should therefore be treated as part of a reusable engineering library. It provides a foundation on which Mintaka can build FPGA and SDR projects for SatCom and other communication applications.

Mintaka Research Library
Mintaka can publicly demonstrate technical depth through a Research Library containing reference algorithms, reference architectures and research concepts.

These do not need to be represented as completed customer projects. Conceptual work should be clearly labeled as Research Concept, Reference Architecture or Proposed Engineering Approach.

Each research item can show:
Problem → Engineering Approach → Reference Algorithm → FPGA Architecture → Verification Strategy → Simulation/Measurement Method → Expected or Measured Results → Applications.

A strong initial area is adaptive signal processing: adaptive filters, interference cancellation, signal detection and adaptive receiver algorithms.

Hardware Prototyping
Mintaka moves beyond simulation when needed. Research algorithms can be validated using SDR hardware such as Pluto/Pluto Plus and FPGA development platforms, together with RF measurement and analysis equipment.

The hardware itself is not the product. Its purpose is to prove that an algorithm or architecture works outside simulation and to provide measurable evidence before a customer commits to a full product implementation.

Copernicus — Internal AI Engineering Coworker
Copernicus is the core internal AI engineering platform supporting Mintaka's SDR, SatCom and FPGA work.

Copernicus should not initially be positioned as the product Mintaka is trying to sell. It is an internal engineering advantage.

The operating model is:
Engineer + Copernicus AI Coworker + Mintaka Research Library + SDR/FPGA Laboratory.

Copernicus acts as an AI coworker specialized around SDR, SatCom, FPGA and engineering research. It assists engineers with research, modeling, experiment preparation, algorithm exploration, verification workflows, analysis of engineering data, documentation and reuse of internal knowledge.

The purpose is not to replace the engineer. The engineer owns technical judgment and responsibility; Copernicus increases the amount of research and engineering work the team can perform and helps make the process reproducible.

This creates an internal technology stack:

Customer Engineering Problem
↓
Mintaka Engineer + Copernicus
↓
Research Library / Models / Reference Algorithms
↓
Simulation & Data Analysis
↓
FPGA Verification / Implementation
↓
SDR & Hardware Prototype
↓
Measured, documented engineering result

Strategic Identity
Mintaka should not present itself as 'an AI company that also does FPGA.'

It should present itself as a serious SDR/SatCom/FPGA engineering company whose engineers happen to have advanced internal AI tooling.

The external story is engineering.
The internal advantage is Copernicus.
The commercial engine is FPGA verification.
The specialization is SDR and SatCom.
The differentiation is research, modeling, adaptive algorithms, data analysis and hardware validation.
