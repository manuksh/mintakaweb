About Mintaka

Mintaka is an SDR, SatCom and FPGA engineering company. We provide specialized engineering services in FPGA design and verification, SDR and satellite communication systems, signal-processing research, communication-system modeling, data analysis and hardware prototyping.

We are not a generic outsourcing or staffing provider. Mintaka works as a specialized engineering and R&D partner — we take clearly defined technical problems and deliver verified, measurable engineering results.

Research Is a Service

We do not need to sell a finished modem, satellite payload or complete communications product — we sell engineering research itself.

From Question to Result

A customer brings a technical question or problem. Mintaka builds the appropriate model or experiment, runs simulations and measurements, analyzes the resulting data and provides an engineering conclusion, recommendations, algorithms or a prototype.

  • Model or experiment design
  • Simulations & measurements
  • Engineering conclusion & deliverable
Example Investigations

Typical studies we can run for a customer:

  • Link behavior under noise, Doppler, interference, fading and synchronization errors

The customer buys the engineering result and reduced technical risk. Models, simulation infrastructure, analysis tools and AI-assisted workflows are the machinery we use to produce it.

  • Statistical analysis & performance measurement
