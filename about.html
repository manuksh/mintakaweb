About Mintaka

Mintaka is an FPGA-first engineering company focused on FPGA and SoC design, SDR systems, functional verification, and embedded software for hardware platforms. We help teams move from architecture and RTL to simulation, implementation, timing closure, board bring-up, and reliable product integration.

FPGA & SoC Design

RTL design, reusable IP, AXI/AXI4-Stream interfaces, clock/reset architecture, CDC strategy, FPGA implementation, and SoC FPGA integration.

Verification & Validation

We build coverage-driven verification environments using SystemVerilog, UVM, assertions, cocotb, regression automation, hardware validation, and disciplined debug workflows.

SDR & RF Systems

FPGA-based DSP pipelines for SDR, RF, and telecommunications systems, including filtering, synchronization, modulation, packet processing, and bring-up support.
